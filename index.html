About

Hi, I’m Sara — the hands behind The Basket Bar Co.

I started this small gifting studio because the most thoughtful gifts are never just picked from a shelf. They are composed with care, chosen with intention, and made to feel personal.

Every basket is hand-tied to order using treats sourced locally and Canadian-made favourites where possible. Whether it’s a get-well surprise, a thank-you for a school committee, a holiday staff gift, or a corporate basket, each one is built around the person it’s for.

No two baskets are ever exactly the same — and that’s the point.

My goal is to make gifting feel easy, thoughtful, and personal — whether it’s one basket or a special order for a whole team.

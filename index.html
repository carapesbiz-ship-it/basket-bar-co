About

Meet Sara — the hands behind Basket Bar Co.

Hi, I’m Sara. I started this small gifting studio because the most thoughtful gifts are never just picked from a shelf. They are composed with care, chosen with intention, and made to feel personal.

Every basket is hand-tied to order using treats sourced locally and Canadian-made favourites where possible. Whether it’s a get-well surprise, a thank-you for a school committee, a holiday staff gift, or a corporate basket, each one is built around the person it’s for.

No two baskets are ever exactly the same — and that’s the point.

My goal is to make gifting feel easy, thoughtful, and personal — whether it’s one basket or a special order for a whole team.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions.

How far in advance should I order a custom gift basket?

We recommend 5–7 days notice for the most thoughtful result. We always do our best to accommodate rush orders for get-well, sympathy, and last-minute corporate gifts in the Lower Mainland — just mention your date in the inquiry and we’ll confirm right away.

What areas do you deliver to?

We hand-deliver across the Lower Mainland, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Moody, Port Coquitlam, New Westminster, Surrey, Langley, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, and Richmond. Local pickup from Coquitlam is also available by appointment.

How much do custom gift baskets cost?

Most baskets fall between $75 and $200, with options under $75 for smaller gestures and $200+ for corporate or volume orders. We build to your budget — share what you’d like to spend and we’ll compose the most thoughtful basket within it.

Can I order corporate gift baskets for my team or clients?

Yes. Corporate gifting is one of our most-requested services — from a handful of thank-you baskets to fifty matching holiday gifts for staff and clients. We offer volume pricing, coordinated themes, branded ribbon or tags, and consolidated delivery across the Lower Mainland.

Can you accommodate allergies, dietary restrictions, or preferences?

Yes. Many of our baskets include food, but every basket is custom — so vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, kosher-style, alcohol-free, and other preferences are easy to accommodate. Just note them in your inquiry.

Can baskets include wine or beer?

Yes — wine and beer baskets are some of our most popular. We feature Canadian wines and BC-brewed beer wherever possible. Alcohol baskets are delivered to recipients 19+ only, and a signature may be required at the door.

Will I see the basket before it’s delivered?

Yes. Once your basket is composed, we send a photo for your approval before it’s wrapped and tied. If you’d like a small adjustment, we’ll make it. We also gently photograph each finished basket before it goes out the door.

Do you ship outside of BC?

Not at this time. We keep delivery local so every basket arrives perfectly composed. If you’re out-of-province and want to send a basket to someone in the Lower Mainland, we can arrange local delivery to their address.

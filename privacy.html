Privacy Policy.

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Who we are

The Basket Bar Co. is a small gifting studio based in Coquitlam, BC, Canada, serving the Lower Mainland. You can reach us at basketbarco@gmail.com.

What information we collect

When you submit an inquiry through this website, we collect only the information you choose to share with us, which may include:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone)
  • The basket style, occasion, budget, theme, and date you’re interested in
  • Allergy or dietary preferences (if you provide them)
  • Pickup or delivery preference and delivery city or postal code
  • Any message or context you write in the inquiry

How we use your information

Your details are used only to reply to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, to prepare and deliver your basket. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

How we store your information

Inquiries are submitted through Formspree, a third-party form service that emails your message to us. Once received, your information is stored in our email account and (where relevant) in our customer notes. We keep it only as long as needed to fulfill your request and any follow-up correspondence.

Cookies and analytics

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. These services may set basic technical cookies. We do not run additional advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

Your rights

Under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. To make a request, email basketbarco@gmail.com and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
